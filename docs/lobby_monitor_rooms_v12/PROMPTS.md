# 모니터룸 로비 컨셉 2종 v12

- 사용자 요청: 첨부한 다중 모니터 비밀방 컨셉의 방 2장 추가.
- 내장 image_gen 사용. 첫 참고 이미지는 방의 컨셉/분위기, 두 번째 v10 이미지는 기존 카툰 그림체의 참고.
- 최종 그림은 픽셀아트가 아닌 굵고 매끈한 선과 플랫 셰이딩.
- A STATIC LOUNGE: 낮은 시점의 모니터 관제실, 왼쪽 메뉴 여백.
- B REWIND ROOM: 높은 모서리 시점의 기억 편집실, 오른쪽 메뉴 여백.
- 두 장 모두 야간 비밀 정신/기억 시술 시설 설정을 유지하며, 독립 원화로 저장. 게임 화면 적용 없음.

## A — STATIC LOUNGE

Use case: stylized-concept.
Generate ONE finished 16:9 widescreen illustrated lobby background, target 2560x1440.
INPUT ROLES: Image 1 (dark pixel-art monitor den) is the ROOM CONCEPT AND MOOD reference: a small enclosed secret room, mismatched screens surrounding the viewer, loose cables, low seats, intimate nighttime monitoring atmosphere. Image 2 (MOOD LAUNDROMAT cartoon) is the DRAWING STYLE reference: smooth bold plum contours, designed rounded/angular geometric forms, crisp flat cel shading and appealing simplified yet reasonably rich equipment. Create a new room; do not copy exact layouts or any text.
IMPORTANT: final image is the clean CARTOON NETWORK / Panty & Stocking inspired thick-line flat-shading style, NOT PIXEL ART and not gritty American comic realism. Smooth high-resolution outlines, no pixel edges, no grain, stipple, hatching, concrete scratches, photoreal reflections or 3D render.
WORLD: back rooms of a clandestine illegal neural/memory treatment clinic, secretly operated after midnight. Computers, visible actual power/data wires, practical equipment and comfortable seating, modest near-future tech. Eccentric and cozy-undercover, no sinister horror or restraints.
PALETTE: darker and more intimate than the bright laundromat: smoky blue and periwinkle walls, plum/indigo shadow shapes, moderately luminous cyan/teal monitors with muted pink/lilac top bars, and just one tiny apricot lamp. Keep the user's softer harmonious contrast: colored shadows instead of pure black, moderate saturation, no fluorescent neon overload, no gray washed-out filter. Screen light forms a few broad flat blue pools. Monitor graphics are bold simple waveforms, memory-node icons, stylized landscape thumbnail blocks and a few abstract rows, NOT paragraphs of microscopic text or a full game UI.
DENSITY: interesting layered cluster of screens, wires and chairs like image 1, simplified into readable medium-size shapes like image 2. 7–9 mismatched screens are enough, avoid dozens of tiny devices. Actual thick looping wires and grouped cable runs should connect computers/screens and disappear into conduit. Each cluster is separated by quiet wall/floor. Slightly jaunty perspective but no extreme Dutch tilt.
MENU NEED: one contiguous SIDE region about 25% of the canvas stays calm, low contrast and low detail, mainly a shadowed wall/partition from roughly 20–80% image height. Make it a natural part of the room, not a blank UI panel. No signage, bright screen spill, cable clusters or shelves in this region. No UI/buttons/currencies, no safe-zone labels.
One coherent full-frame illustration, no people or human silhouettes, no outside series logos, no watermark, no split panels, no collage. Only the physical room name specified below is readable English; no other prominent text.

CAMERA: low relaxed seated-eye view from an offset doorway looking toward an angled wall of screens on the BACK RIGHT; not an elevated laundromat room view. A slight edge of the offset doorway frames only the far left perimeter and top. Keep the central lower floor moderately open, not busy square tiles. A pleasant secret-screen-den concept with design richness concentrated at right.
