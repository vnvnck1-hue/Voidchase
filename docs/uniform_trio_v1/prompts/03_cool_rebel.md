Use case: stylized-concept. Produce ONE finished full-body 2D game character sprite with genuine transparent background.
References 1–4 are the equally authoritative game art references for anatomy, face, simplified shapes, proportions, ink and cel rendering. Reference 5 is ONLY the school-uniform concept cue; design a DISTINCT new character rather than recoloring her.
Keep the same cast proportions: main hair crown to chin = H, crown to lowest sole around 2.72H, within 2.67–2.80H. Exclude separate ponytails and ornaments from main head. Rounded broad cheek/jaw, broad main head, enormous expressive eyes, tiny nose and simple mouth. Small torso, short neck, compact substantial arms and legs, readable hands and chunky shoes matching the original four refs. Main head height is about 37% of crown-to-sole height. Shoulders just below chin ~1.09H, waist ~1.43H, pelvis ~1.85H, knees ~2.26H. Construct naturally; do not stretch or compress an existing figure. No elongated anime anatomy or ultra-tiny toddler body.
Hand-drawn pressure-sensitive black ink is essential: strong bold silhouettes, varied thickness WITHIN each long stroke, thicker at overlaps, thin tapered interior folds, visible fine dry-brush tooth at ink edges. Controlled natural hand-inked curves, not perfect vector monoline. Large flat colors, selective simple one-step cel shadows and few chunky highlights. No airbrush gradients or blanket noise filter. Small-scale readability and original cast's moderate detail density.
School uniform wardrobe is neat and age-appropriate: collared blouse, blazer or cardigan layer, pleated skirt, opaque socks or tights, loafers. No sexualized pose, cleavage, exposed underwear or body emphasis.
One single character centered, isolated on true alpha transparency, full hair, all hands and shoe soles completely visible with ample clear margins. No glow, ground shadow, background, labels, text, guide lines, variants or character sheet.
