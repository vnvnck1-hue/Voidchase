DESIGN:
A compact hovering "many-eyed celestial gyroscope". Body consists of THREE intact substantial circular bands crossing at different angles: ONE broad dominant outer annulus tilted diagonally in three-quarter perspective; ONE smaller vertical ring visible inside; ONE small near-horizontal cross ring linking the inner volume. Round continuous band silhouettes with restrained broad faceted rims, not jagged spikes or floating plate shards. Ring overlaps and occlusion must be easy to follow. Keep two or three LARGE CLEAR see-through openings through the body; background is visible through them. This must read as a ring mechanism with an empty center, not a sphere whose surface has ring patterns.

Color the bands muted antique GOLD / OCHRE with warm pale-gold top planes, bronze and purple-indigio shadow planes and a few teal inset grooves. Keep the new reference's gold identity while fitting the violet battlefield. A few large simple crescent/triangle engravings on unoccupied areas, no dense ornamental noise.

Attach around 24–28 raised ROUND IVORY EYEBALLS with small coral-red CIRCULAR pupils across the surfaces of the rings, including dense small clusters on the visible outer front band and a few on the inner receding bands. Both wings fully in frame. Soft feather wings, not crystal blades, no extra wing pairs.
