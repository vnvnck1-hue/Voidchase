NEW DESIGN, "fractured void witness":
A compact hovering irregular polyhedral seed / geometric reliquary, slightly pointed at the lower tip. Its dark indigo-purple triangular shell has split into FIVE broad interlocking plates that levitate a short distance apart, revealing dark empty gaps. A few inset muted teal triangular enamel patches recall the source, but dark violet dominates. One large almond-shaped opening in the near front-left plate reveals a luminous magenta vertical-pupil EYE suspended in black emptiness; pale lilac eye-rim, no protruding white eyeball. FIVE smaller watchful eyes, simple pale-lavender shapes with pink slit pupils, are recessed in selected side/top plates, following their planes and perspective. Eye clusters are sparse and deliberately composed so the large eye is dominant at small game size. The main eye watches the party on the left.
TWO small wing-like fans near the upper body are constructed from THREE detached broad curved ivory-lavender crystal feathers EACH. These blade-feathers float with small dark gaps between them, broken and slightly asymmetric; they keep the whimsical little-wing silhouette of source but are unmistakably supernatural void appendages, not literal yellow bird wings. Near fan is larger, far fan partly hidden and foreshortened. No giant sharp crystal mane and no all-over tiny spikes. One loose small feather shard drifts below a fan. Shell, eye and wing fans form an unusually elegant compact silhouette, uncanny yet cartoon-readable. No crown, no halo, no hanging ribbons, no limbs, no human face, no mouth. Let the controlled gaps and quiet glowing eye carry the design instead of excessive ornament.
