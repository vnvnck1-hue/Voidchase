DESIGN:
One hovering many-eyed "void witness" creature. Compact gently asymmetrical twenty-sided body, roughly round overall with a pointed lower vertex and only a few large, clearly designed triangular faces. Dark desaturated teal shell, turquoise upper faces, deep indigo undersides, simple violet inset panel seams. Keep the teal identity from image 1; a restrained tiny magenta void glow comes from selected panel gaps, not dozens of purple crystal spikes. One dominant large watchful eye on the near front-left facet, smaller satellite eyes around it, fewer smaller eyes on the receding upper/right facets. Coral-red / crimson pupils turned toward the player's LEFT.
