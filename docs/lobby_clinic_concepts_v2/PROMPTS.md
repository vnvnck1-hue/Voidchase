# 로비 상담소 — American Comic Book Style / v2

사용자 요청: 영어 이름, 유머러스하고 키치한 인상, 미국 코믹북 스타일, 과장되고 재미있는 구도. 서로 다른 컨셉 2장.

내장 image_gen 사용. 첨부된 실내 배경 2장은 스타일·조명·원근 참고 자료로 사용하며, 원본 로고와 캐릭터는 사용하지 않는다. 기존 네러티브의 마음 방문·기억 회복에 사용자가 추가한 불법 야간 시술 설정을 반영했다. 게임 화면 적용은 하지 않는다.

## A — HEAD CASE

‘골칫덩이 / 머리를 다루는 가게’의 중의적 농담. 낮은 시점과 기울어진 원근의 뒷골목 외관.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE finished original 16:9 widescreen illustration, target 2560x1440, for a game's lobby background. AMERICAN COMIC BOOK STYLE, with the supplied images as visual style and composition references only. No copying their characters, logos, room layout or posters.
Visual language: VERY expressive cartoon perspective, dramatically exaggerated foreground-to-background scale, angular wonky architecture, bold varied-weight ink outlines, large flat cel-shaded black/navy shadow masses, sparse gritty halftone print texture and dry-brush edges, limited vivid neon color blocks, dramatic diagonal light shapes. Emphasize the energetic graphic American action-cartoon/comic sensibility in the references. NOT anime, not realistic architectural rendering, not softly painted generic game fantasy, not 3D. Architecture and camera must have personality and playful skew. Empty environments can feel energetic through composition alone.
Narrative: a clandestine facility using illegal mind-entry and memory-restoration technology for mental treatment, run discreetly and opening only at night. A team of maid helpers enters clients' inner landscapes to untangle thoughts and retrieve good memories, with subtle star/constellation connections. It is kitschy, eccentric, slightly shady, and oddly welcoming. Humor comes from its improvised small-business personality. No horrific treatment imagery. Nothing publicly advertises illegal procedures.
No people, no character silhouettes, no game HUD, no menu buttons, no watermark, no copied reference trademarks, no comic panel borders, no collage, no speech bubbles. ONLY ENGLISH lettering. Make the business name readable and integrated naturally into the environment. Keep visual breathing room for future lobby overlays, without drawing overlays.
CONCEPT A: "HEAD CASE" — an absurd little after-hours brain repair shop crammed into a narrow city alley, illustrated as an exterior establishing shot.
Composition: an EXTREME low camera almost at curb height, lively Dutch angle about 12 degrees and exaggerated wide-angle three-point perspective looking UP. A giant foreshortened swinging blade sign in the upper-left foreground reads exactly "HEAD CASE" in chunky offbeat hand-lettered comic capitals, with a simple kitschy head-shaped toolbox emblem. The narrow, crooked two-story clinic leans into the scene in the middle-right, squeezed between looming closed buildings with converging rooflines. Its mismatched drainpipes curl like a cartoon Rube Goldberg contraption. An oversized foreground handrail and curving curb sweep diagonally toward a tiny recessed clinic door. The pavement occupies enough calmer dark space to support future lobby UI. Do not repeat a static straight-on rectangular shop elevation.
Design: shabby retro repair-shop facade, tilted awning, patchwork siding, crooked metal staircase on one side, closed upper windows. A small neon "OPEN" on the recessed door, an old doorbell, blinds. One oblique turquoise window provides a subtle partial glimpse of a comfy reclining treatment chair under a jury-rigged colander-like neural helmet connected to chunky analog electronics, with tiny star indicator lights. Keep the machine a believable humorous mind-treatment prop, not a torture instrument. A secondary small wall placard reads exactly "WALK-INS. WEIRD HOURS." This humor is aimed at the shop and its odd opening hours.
Lighting: midnight indigo and ink-black shadow masses; aggressive acidic lime beam from the clinic slicing diagonally across the alley; hot purple rim light, electric cyan in the entrance and a few punchy coral accents. The signs are small-business signs in a hidden alley, not a huge public hospital billboard. Graphic lighting and strong silhouette design; simplified bold forms and expressive inky edges. Slightly mischievous rather than menacing. One cohesive scene filling the canvas.

Composition: a lively HIGH CORNER VIEW looking diagonally down into a long trapezoid-shaped room, strongly skewed cartoon perspective with a slight Dutch tilt.
